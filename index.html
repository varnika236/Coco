What is Coco?

Technology that turns concern into action.

Coco is a community-driven platform designed to make reporting and helping animals easier.

Instead of simply seeing an animal in need and wondering what to do, Coco gives you a simple way to report the situation and connect useful information with people who can help.

See an animal that needs help?
Report it. Share the details. Let the community take it forward.
How it works
